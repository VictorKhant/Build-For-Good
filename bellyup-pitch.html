The gap

Food recovery serves Costco.
Not the corner kitchen.

  • Big-box programs exist — mega-supermarkets have pickup contracts and compliance teams.
  • Small restaurants already give — informally, untracked, unreceipted. No paper trail means no tax deduction, and no reason to keep going.
  • The long tail is expensive — many small pickups across many locations is exactly the routing problem nonprofits can't afford to solve by hand.

The engine

One honest equation.

net = reward − cost
reward = meals served × $/meal × access boost + overflow × ½
cost = minutes × $17.75/hr + miles × $0.76/mi

Every constant is sourced, not invented: San Diego minimum wage (2026) · IRS standard mileage rate (2026) · Feeding America's 1.2 lb-per-meal conversion. Change a constant, change the policy — the model is an argument you can audit.

Fairness, engineered

No block gets ten dinners
while the next gets none.

  • Serving limits — two deliveries per hotspot per night, hard cap. A block whose need is met leaves the candidate pool entirely.
  • Remaining-need scoring — a half-served block only earns reward on what's still unmet.
  • Access-gap boost — up to +50% reward for blocks whose only food access is a monthly pantry. Scarcity is weighted, not just proximity.
