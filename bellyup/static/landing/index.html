How it works

Report, collect, and out the door the same evening

1

A kitchen says what is left

A restaurant, hotel, grocer or venue reports the pounds, what it is, when it can be picked up and how long it keeps. Nothing to donate tonight is a real answer.

2

One collector is asked

An agency box truck or a pantry van, whichever can serve the most people for what the run actually costs. The request goes to that one collector, not to a noticeboard.

3

It goes where people are

Out to a counted block if the crew can get there before nine, or into a pantry that opens tomorrow. Every confirmed run is logged, with a receipt for the donor.

For restaurants

Donating costs you nothing, and it counts at tax time

You do not pay to donate and you do not drive. The collector absorbs the cost of the run, so the only thing asked of a kitchen is a report at the end of the night.

Every confirmed pickup files a receipt in the ledger: pounds, date, who collected, and the estimated fair market value of the food — the paperwork behind an enhanced deduction under IRC §170(e)(3).
